Staffing rules

Saturday: everyone off, no shifts scheduled.

Sunday: 3 workers on 10:00-18:00 (Israel time). Monday-Thursday: all 6 work — 5 on 10:00-18:00 and 1 on 14:00-22:00 (the "US shift", still Israel-time hours, just named for who it serves). That 14:00-22:00 slot rotates through all 6 workers on a running counter across the whole month. Friday: 3 workers — 2 on 10:00-18:00, 1 on 14:00-22:00.

Weekend coverage is split into two fixed trios that alternate whole weekends: Group 1 (Ivan Mladenovski, Djordje Krstic, Aleksandar Trajkovic) and Group 2 (Petar Radakovic, Nikola Ristić, Nikola Petrov). Whichever group is "on" for a given weekend works both that Friday and the following Sunday together, then gets a full break — no weekend duty at all — until their next turn two weeks later. This month: Group 1 covers the weekends of 04/09-06/09 and 18/09-20/09; Group 2 covers 11/09-13/09 and 25/09-27/09. Every week still splits cleanly into one group on Sunday and the other on Friday, so everyone works exactly 5 days that week (4 Mon-Thu days plus one weekend shift).

Because September 1 falls on a Tuesday, the first calendar row only shows Tue-Sat (no Sun/Mon from August), and the last row only shows Sun-Wed (no Thu-Sat from October) — these are partial edge weeks.

Time zones: shift times are defined in Israel local time (UTC+3, Israel Daylight Time applies through late September). Serbia is shown at CEST (UTC+2), US at Eastern Daylight Time (UTC-4), and UTC at +0. Use the "Display time zone" control below to convert every shift shown.

Admin panel: once unlocked, you can pick any custom From/To date range to total up shifts and absences — it defaults to the first working day of August (Aug 2) through the last day of this schedule (Sep 30). August is included for reference only as the default rotation (no manual edits carried over); counts are always capped at today's date so future shifts aren't counted early.
