Rules applied (default rotation): the two working hours are 10:00-18:00 (Israel time) and 14:00-22:00 (US time). Sun = 3 workers, all 10:00-18:00. Mon-Thu = all 6 work (5 on 10:00-18:00 + 1 on 14:00-22:00), the 14:00-22:00 slot rotates day by day. Fri = 3 workers total, 2 on 10:00-18:00 + 1 on 14:00-22:00 - that slot rotates among that week's Friday group. Sat = everyone off.

Every worker works exactly 5 days/week: the 4 Mon-Thu days, plus either Sunday or Friday (never both). Two groups of 3 alternate Sunday vs. Friday duty week to week, and within the Friday group, who takes the 14:00-22:00 slot rotates too - so nobody is stuck with the same weekend or the same US-hours slot every time.

Time zone note: both shift times (10:00-18:00 and 14:00-22:00) are defined in Israel local time - the "US shift" is just the name for the 14:00-22:00 slot, not a US-local time. Use the "Display time zone" control to see the same shifts converted into Serbia, US, or UTC time. Conversions assume August 2026 daylight-saving rules (Israel UTC+3, Serbia/CEST UTC+2, US Eastern/EDT UTC-4, UTC+0) - flag me if the US side isn't Eastern time and I'll adjust.
